# V20 · Hindi-first bilingual OneShotGS branding

Chosen visual: logo concept #1 (target-arrow and book, bilingual purple/gold
badge). Splash and app header show both **बीएसएससी सीजीएल 4 फ्री मॉक**
and **BSSC CGL 4 Free Mock**, with OneShotGS attribution.

The large number is clearly stated as a goal in Hindi:
**“लक्ष्य: 15,000 प्रश्न”** (100 planned mocks × 150 questions).
It is not a representation of presently available or validated inventory.
Source repository currently bundles four starter papers, with repeated
questions noted in the [V16 audit](V16_QUESTION_BANK_AUDIT.md).

Students can clearly switch languages in the Home header, on the Home
welcome card, or in Profile. The language toggle changes bilingual app
labels and questions; device-language-localized launcher label uses Hindi
on Hindi-language devices.

`Balanced` / toughness chip was removed from the mock catalogue;
legacy `difficulty` metadata is kept in Firebase for compatibility,
without being displayed.
