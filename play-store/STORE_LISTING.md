Short description:

Full description:

Prepare for BSSC CGL 4 with OneShotGS, an independent Hindi-English mock test practice app for aspirants.

PRACTICE WITH PURPOSE
• Take available BSSC CGL-style mock tests in Hindi or English.
• Review answers, explanations and your scores after each test.
• Track your progress, accuracy and weak subjects.
• See your attempt history and compare scores in practice leaderboards.
• Get optional exam reminders when dates or new mocks are published.

FREE PRACTICE, SUPPORTED BY ADS
OneShotGS uses advertising to keep practice accessible. Some mock tests can be unlocked by watching rewarded ads. There are no advertisements inside the timed question experience.

YOUR STUDENT PROFILE
Create a profile with Google sign-in and your chosen name and Indian mobile number. Phone numbers are self-entered, not verified by an SMS OTP. WhatsApp updates require a separate optional consent.

INDEPENDENT PREPARATION APP
OneShotGS is NOT an official BSSC app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by the Bihar Staff Selection Commission or the Government of Bihar. Always verify official exam dates, eligibility rules, syllabi and notices on official BSSC channels. The mock questions are practice material and not an official question paper.
