# V16 space-efficiency refinement

Home has a single horizontally scrollable row of four real animated metrics, instead of a two-row stat grid. This saves roughly one card-row of vertical space on narrow Android phones.

Home content order: greeting, officer hero, Firebase promotional image carousel (if configured), daily mission, clearly labeled AdMob banner, countdown and practice links. A second banner remains near the end of the scrolling Home content. No ad is shown during the timed exam.

The bottom bar remains a compact 56dp floating surface. Attribution is accessible in Profile and the global branding header is removed during timed tests and results.

On-device impression, scroll and accidental-click behavior still requires QA; this layout isn't a guarantee of monetization, Play approval or specified frame rates.
